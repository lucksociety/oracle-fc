# Full-day men's Polymarket fixture inventory

The November 11 scan showed seven remaining men's fixtures, omitting Vietnam–Chinese Taipei at 07:00 ET. Direct Polymarket inventory found eight matches for the New York date. Discovery also incorrectly used the model's configured league list as its gender/competition allowlist, so an unsupported men's international could disappear instead of being shown as unmodeled.

Use Polymarket's date-bounded soccer inventory as the daily fixture source. Query open and closed events explicitly, paginate both fully and deduplicate parent match IDs. Reject prop/halftime/exact-score/first-scorer child events and outrights. Scheduled startTime determines the New York date; event creation dates, settlement deadlines and stale dates in rescheduled slugs do not. The clean fixture is an identity-only allowlist: event ID, two teams, competition, subcategory, kickoff, slug and source. Scores, live statuses, probabilities, prices, books and volume never enter the engine or clean cache. The inventory provider is independent of the later Jupiter price comparison.

Men-only discovery uses reviewed Polymarket soccer competition codes (2026-11-11 registry) plus existing configured men's codes; women's codes and women's descriptors in teams, competition or tags are rejected. Taiwan Mulan and NWSL are explicitly excluded despite not containing the word Women. New unclassified codes fail closed pending classification. Discovery acceptance never implies model support: FIFA Friendlies are visible, but the domestic-club model still returns unsupported for future national-team fixtures.

The daily inventory now includes started and completed games. If no forecast was frozen before kickoff, display an explicit no-pregame-forecast row and never call the simulation or create a wager for it. Preserve genuinely saved pregame forecasts and immutable hashes for earlier games. Keep the existing kickoff, quote freshness, score agreement, >50% and positive fee-inclusive EV rules. No scoring coefficients or model probabilities are changed.

Discord's default all-games card reports fixture count, predictions, data/coverage gaps and earlier games without a saved pregame prediction. Only date files explicitly tagged full-day-men-v1 are labeled full-day inventory; legacy caches and fallback data are not. Explicit bets-only filtering remains available; the default is the complete card.

Validation covers both open/closed pagination, deduplication, partial-fetch cache rejection, women's/prop exclusion, stale slug dates, ET midnight boundaries and the 25-hour daylight-saving day. Tests ensure earlier fixtures cannot call the engine or be backfilled, genuine pregame forecasts remain immutable, and an eight-game Discord card displays five predictions, two gaps and one earlier unforecast game. Real November 11 identity refresh returned all eight reviewed men's fixtures. Tests and model/market firewall checks run without posting to Discord.
