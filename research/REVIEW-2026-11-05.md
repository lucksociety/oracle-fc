## Shipped corrections

The selector recorded thin quotes but did not exclude them. Both Python and the shared browser evaluator now require a two-sided, uncrossed quote within 10 cents, positive finite traded volume, and explicit 90-minute-plus-stoppage settlement. Period markets are excluded. Volume is a liquidity screen and does not prove executable depth at the ask.

A snapshot used one batch-start timestamp for every event. Each event now records when its response arrives. That individual timestamp controls kickoff and freeze comparisons. A five-minute age limit rejects stale and future quotes, and Discord rechecks age and scheduled kickoff before displaying a new recommendation. Historical frozen records remain available for grading.

Match-result bets expose the two main component probabilities and EV at the lower estimate. This is a range of model estimates, not a confidence interval. The browser's match-result agreement rule now matches the server.

These are selection, evidence and execution corrections. The core forecast coefficients remain those supported by the existing chronological evaluation.
