# Zero-bet forecast display and women's coverage

The November 10 Discord run 42870082616 contained 17 fixtures, zero usable forecasts and zero bets. Five games had already kicked off without a frozen forecast; the other twelve lacked sufficiently rated team history. Predictions were not being hidden by the betting selector. The original artifact is retained separately; no historical forecast or Discord result is invented.

Keep all valid score/1X2/xG forecasts visible when no bets qualify. An explicitly bets-only view now falls back to available predictions if its entire card is a pass. When no forecast exists, distinguish a coverage gap from a price/EV pass and identify missing team history or missing pregame snapshots. Expired or absent quotes do not hide forecasts. No bet criteria or underlying men's model coefficients are relaxed.

Add public completed regulation results from ESPN's Women's Champions League, Women's Super League and French women's top division. All women's competitions share a separate Women:espn-ID identity namespace; the women's results engine is isolated from both the primary men's model and the existing men's supplemental engine. Explicit fixture aliases were checked against those competition records. Women's cross-competition fixtures use the women's Champions League environment. Men's namesakes can never supply women's ratings.

Women's outputs are marked forecast-only and cannot produce recommendations in either the comparison engine or the Discord renderer until betting calibration is validated. The existing minimum of eight weighted matches per club, latest result within sixty days, strict date cutoff and pregame freeze safeguards remain. This is new descriptive coverage, not a demonstrated accuracy/profit improvement; no player-lineup adjustments are claimed for this supplemental model.

Live source replay produced 977 completed regulation results over 2024–2026. Of the three later Women's Champions League games in the original card, PSG–Bayern had enough history for a provisional forecast: modal score 1–1, 1X2 approximately 35%/25%/40%, expected goals 1.53–1.63. Roma–Servette and Inter–Manchester City still failed the history threshold and remain unforecast. Lower-division/cup opponents lacking rated history remain unavailable. No retrospective forecasts were created for already-started matches.

A local report replay with prices suppressed showed 17 games, zero bets and one score prediction, explicitly labelled forecast-only. No Discord post was sent. A local pregame snapshot used for this validation is retained with the review outputs; deployment relies on normal fresh-command ingestion and freezing, not on publishing a simulated historical card.

Validation: 86 Python tests, nine Discord test files, seven prospective-learning tests and evergreen checks (one expected skip). New regressions cover gender/identity isolation, forecast-only status, missing-history explanations, all-pass cards, bets-only fallback and rejection of an erroneously cached bet on a forecast-only game. Pipeline push triggers now include forecast/configuration changes so new coverage is ingested automatically.
